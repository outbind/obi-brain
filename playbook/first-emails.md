# Writing first emails that get replies

General notes for people and AI agents. They're plain, common-sense basics, not a formula.

## Before you write

1. **Know who you're writing to.** Know their role, their company and one real thing about them. If you can't name that one thing, research more before you write.
2. **Know why now.** A new hire, a launch, a move or a post they wrote. A reason to write today beats a clever line.
3. **Pick one problem.** Choose the one most likely to matter to this person, not everything your product does.

## The email

4. **Keep it short.** Aim for under 90 words, readable in ten seconds on a phone.
5. **Write plain text.** No images, no fancy layout, at most one link.
6. **Open with them, not you.** Start the first line with something about them. Never "I hope this finds you well" or "My name is".
7. **Say the problem in their words.** Write "chasing late invoices", not "accounts receivable optimisation".
8. **Make one claim you can back up.** Don't stack adjectives. If you can't prove it, cut it.
9. **Ask one easy question.** "Worth a look?" is easier to answer than "Can we book 30 minutes Tuesday?"
10. **Sound like a person.** Use contractions and short sentences. Read it out loud, and if you wouldn't say it, rewrite it.
11. **Write a boring subject.** Two to four lower-case words, the way a colleague would write it.
12. **Never fake familiarity.** No "Re:" on a first email, no "as we discussed", no made-up mutual friends.

## Follow-ups

13. **Send two or three, spaced out.** Wait three to five days, then about a week.
14. **Add something new each time.** A different angle, a useful link or a short example. Never "just bumping this".
15. **Make the last one easy to close.** "Should I stop writing?" gets honest answers.

## Respect

16. **Write to one person at a time.** If the email could go to anyone, it will be treated like it was sent to everyone.
17. **Make opting out easy, and honour it at once.**
18. **Don't guess email addresses.** Verify them first. Bounces hurt every email you send after.
19. **Send at a human hour** in their time zone, on a sensible number per day per mailbox.
20. **Never invent proof.** No fake customers, numbers or quotes. One true sentence beats three impressive ones.
